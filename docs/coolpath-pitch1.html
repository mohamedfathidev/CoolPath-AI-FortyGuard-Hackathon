CoolPath AI

A heat-aware walking route planner and urban heat-island map for Phoenix.

In a Phoenix summer, walking through the heat isn't a choice — it's a health risk. CoolPath makes it navigable.

The problem

Heat is a mobility barrier.

13+ hrs/day above 95°F
  • Regular maps only optimize distance — routing people straight down the hottest, most sun-exposed corridors.
  • Climate change makes every Phoenix summer hotter and longer, widening the gap between safe and dangerous routes.
The solution

Three ways in, one heat model.

01 · Map

Plan a route

Origin, destination, time → the shortest route and a heat-optimized route side by side, with the exact trade-off in distance and heat avoided.

02 · Agent

Ask the agent

A plain-language brief drives an autonomous AI agent that geocodes, pulls heat data, routes, and explains — source-cited, streamed live.

03 · Climate

Heat islands

A city-wide map colouring every block by daily hours of extreme heat — naming the hottest zones and where cooling matters most.

The opportunity

The coolest route — a gap no map fills.

  • Every navigation app optimizes one thing: distance. None account for a rider or walker's heat exposure.
  • CoolPath is the decision layer between temperature data and the people — and infrastructure — exposed to it.
  • Runs on data cities already have. No new sensors, no hardware — just the FortyGuard API and a map.
Measurable outcome

Every route comes with a concrete number: the hours of extreme-heat exposure avoided — a metric institutions can report and act on.

FortyGuard Temperature API

Real data, everywhere it matters.

POST /v1/heatmap · tcmInstantaneous temperature — shown to the user
POST /v1/heatmap · exceedanceDaily heat-exposure hours — weights the routing engine and powers the heat-island map
POST /v1/env_paramsHeat index, humidity, air quality — the "why this block is hot"
POST /v1/heat_intelligenceDeep-dive report — the agent's premium analysis
GET /v1/status · creditsAsync submit-and-poll + self-limiting on credit usage

Cached in Postgres · keys stay server-side · never re-spend a credit on an identical query.

Impact

Built for who owns the sidewalk.

Decision-makers

  • Transit agencies — heat-aware first/last-mile
  • Universities & large campuses
  • City walkability & climate offices

Beyond the app

  • Integration API — a drop-in coolest-route service for delivery & mobility apps
  • Heat-island map — shows cities where shade & cooling deliver the most protection
What's next

From pilot to platform.

  • Multi-city expansion — the architecture generalizes to any FortyGuard-covered U.S. city.
  • Published SDK + authed API — a real B2B routing service with keys and rate limiting.
  • Monitoring agent — scheduled heat sweeps and alerts for a corridor or campus.
  • Cooling-infrastructure planning — layer tree-canopy & shade data to model interventions.

CoolPath doesn't just route around heat — it maps the climate risk.
